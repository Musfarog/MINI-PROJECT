This project focuses on analyzing customer purchasing behavior, campaign performance, and spending patterns using Power BI. The dashboard provides business insights that help understand customer engagement, regional performance, and overall sales trends.

Objective

The main objective of this project is to:

Analyze customer purchase behavior
Evaluate marketing campaign performance
Identify top-performing countries
Monitor customer web engagement
Compare spending across product categories
Generate business insights for decision-making
Tools & Technologies Used

Total Revenue

Key Insights
1. Spain Generated the Highest Campaign Acceptance

Spain recorded the highest number of accepted campaigns compared to other countries, indicating stronger customer engagement and marketing effectiveness.

2. Sales Peaked in 2013

Customer purchasing activity reached its highest point in 2013 before declining in 2014.

3. Low Complaint Volume

The dashboard shows relatively low customer complaints, suggesting positive customer satisfaction.

4. Regional Sales Imbalance

Business performance is highly dependent on a few countries, especially Spain, while several regions show lower engagement levels.

5. Spending Patterns Vary Across Categories

Customer spending is concentrated more heavily in selected product categories, indicating differences in customer preferences.

Business Conclusion

The analysis suggests that marketing campaigns performed strongly in specific regions while other markets require improved engagement strategies. The dashboard also highlights changing customer purchase behavior and spending trends over time.
